# YAFA VANAM Kind Beauty and Giving Policy

Status: Owner-approved for implementation
Approved: 29 August 2026
Public effective date: 1 September 2026

## Cruelty-Free*

YAFA VANAM does not conduct or commission animal testing on its finished cosmetic products. We require direct formula and ingredient suppliers not to conduct animal testing specifically for YAFA VANAM.

YAFA VANAM is not currently certified by a third-party cruelty-free organization.

*Cruelty-Free is a YAFA VANAM policy claim, not a third-party certification. It is based on the brand's practices and supplier declarations available to YAFA VANAM. If a market requires animal testing by law, YAFA VANAM will not knowingly make a cruelty-free claim for products sold under that requirement without a separate public disclosure.*

## Vegan* where marked

Not every YAFA VANAM product is automatically claimed as vegan. Only a product displaying the Vegan* designation has been reviewed against the current formula and available supplier declarations for intentionally added animal-derived ingredients.

If the Vegan* designation is absent, the product must be treated as not yet verified rather than described as vegan.

*Vegan means the reviewed formula contains no intentionally added animal-derived ingredients according to the final formula and supplier information available at the time of review. It is not a statement about allergy suitability, shared manufacturing equipment, or third-party vegan certification unless a certification is separately named.*

## 1% for Nature and Animal Care*

Beginning 1 September 2026, YAFA VANAM will allocate 1% of eligible net online product sales to registered nonprofit partners working in animal welfare, habitat restoration, reforestation, or community environmental care.

The program uses a rotating group of vetted partners rather than one permanent charity. Recipient names and allocations will be published in an annual impact statement. The first reporting period runs from 1 September 2026 through 31 August 2027.

*Eligible net online product sales means product revenue actually received through YAFA VANAM-owned online channels, after discounts, returns, refunds and chargebacks, and excluding taxes, shipping, duties, gift-card purchases and cancelled orders. Allocation is calculated after each annual reporting period. This is a company-funded commitment and does not add a separate donation charge to the customer's order.*

## Customer-facing approved answers

**Are all YAFA VANAM products cruelty-free? Are there certifications?**
Yes — YAFA VANAM follows its own Cruelty-Free* standard. We do not test finished products on animals or ask suppliers to test specifically for us. We are not currently third-party certified.

**Are all products vegan?**
Only specified products are Vegan*. Look for the Vegan* designation on the product page. If it is not shown, the formula has not yet been verified for that claim.

**Does a sale fund charity?**
Yes. From 1 September 2026, YAFA VANAM allocates 1% of eligible net online product sales to registered animal-welfare and environmental nonprofit partners*. Partners and allocations are disclosed in the annual impact statement.

## Publishing controls

- Preserve the asterisk when using Cruelty-Free*, Vegan*, or the 1% giving claim.
- Do not display a Vegan* badge until the final formula and supplier declarations have been reviewed.
- Do not name a certification unless it is current and documented.
- Do not name a charitable recipient until an agreement or completed allocation is documented.
- Publish the annual impact statement after each reporting period, including recipients, amounts and calculation period.
